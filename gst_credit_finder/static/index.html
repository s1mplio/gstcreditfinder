How do I get GSTR-2B from the GST portal?
  1. Go to www.gst.gov.in and log in.
  2. Open Services → Returns → Returns Dashboard.
  3. Choose the financial year and the month, then click Search.
  4. On the GSTR-2B tile, click Download (or View, then Download).
  5. Click Download JSON, not PDF or Excel. Large returns come as a ZIP; upload it as it is.

GSTR-2B for a month is ready on the 14th of the next month. Add the following months too: a supplier who filed late shows up there, and you'll see that credit as recovered.

Files are not stored. Only results are saved, to show next month's recovery.
