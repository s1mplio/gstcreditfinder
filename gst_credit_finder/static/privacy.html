GST Credit Finder ("we", "the service") is operated by [legal name of your business], [address]. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the service, why, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number or email address, whether you are a shopkeeper or CA, and your password (stored only as a scrypt hash; we cannot read it).
  • Business details: names and GSTINs of the businesses you add.
  • Check results: when you run a check, we save the result (bill-by-bill matching, supplier names and GSTINs, invoice numbers, dates and amounts) and the Excel report, so you can see past checks and what you recovered.
  • Reminder status: what you mark for each supplier (sent, replied, fixed).
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps you logged in, and server logs (IP address, time, page requested) used for security.

What we do not keep

The purchase register and GSTR-2B files you upload are processed in memory and are never written to disk. Only the results described above are saved. We do not ask for or store your GST portal password.

Why we use it

  • To run the GST credit checks you ask for and show you the results.
  • To keep your account secure (login, password reset codes, blocking repeated wrong passwords).
  • To send you a monthly reminder when GSTR-2B is ready, if you leave that switched on. You can turn it off in Account settings.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who else processes it

  • Hosting: [hosting provider], where the service and its database run, in [country/region].
  • Email: [email provider], to deliver password reset codes and reminders sent by email.
  • SMS: [SMS provider, e.g. MSG91], to deliver codes and reminders to mobile numbers.
  • Fonts: pages load the Inter font from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font is downloaded.

Supplier data

Check results include details of your suppliers (business names, GSTINs and invoices), which come from your own records and GSTR-2B. We use them only to produce your results and reminders.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and saved checks until you delete them. Backups of the database are kept for [14 days] and then overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete a single check, a business with all its checks, or your whole account at any time from within the app. Deletion is immediate in the live service and disappears from backups within [14 days].
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it by writing to [contact email].
  • Turn monthly reminders on or off in Account settings.

Security

Passwords are hashed, the session cookie cannot be read by scripts on the page, and each account can only see its own businesses and checks. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Changes
